# Art Direction
- Late-night Japanese mountain pass (touge) downhill, semi-realistic, moody.
- Palette: deep navy sky, dark asphalt, white/yellow road lines, sodium orange accents, pine greens.
- Player: white/black two-tone 1980s Japanese hatchback-coupe. Rival: yellow 1990s rotary sports coupe.
- Textures: asphalt road strip with lines, forest grass slope.
- UI: arcade racing HUD, analog tach, italic digital numbers, red/orange glow.
